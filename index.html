Mission Guide

Select a training tab above. Follow the quick directions below.

01 · Learn

Click a character to hear it and add it to your message, or type a word below. Press Play message to transmit.

02 · Listen & Decode

Press Play signal, listen, then click the matching letter. Use Next signal to continue.

03 · Transmit

Press and hold the on-screen Morse key (or SPACE) to make a tone. Release for silence. Short presses make dots; longer presses make dashes. Select Check My Morse.

04 · Mission Challenge

Press Play signal, type the decoded word into the answer box in the center, then press Enter or Submit.

05 · Live Decoder

Play an audible Morse tone from a separate device or keyer. Tap Start Listening and allow microphone access. Set the tone automatically or manually and adjust detection sensitivity if needed.
